03 / CUSTOMER SEGMENTS

SEGMENTATION

Five customer profiles,
five different priorities.

The framework separates customers by behavior, value and engagement, allowing CRM actions to become more relevant to each group.

NEW — Build familiarity and encourage first use.
ACTIVE — Strengthen engagement and introduce additional value.
HIGH VALUE — Protect loyalty and reinforce long-term value.
AT RISK — Detect declining behavior and trigger retention actions.
CHURNED — Re-engage selectively or learn from customer loss.

LIFECYCLE MARKETING

From first contact
to long-term loyalty.

Each stage creates a different opportunity for CRM to influence behavior, strengthen relationships and reduce friction.

Acquisition — attract the right customer.
Activation — turn sign-up into meaningful first use.
Engagement — deepen usage and expand the relationship.
Retention — respond before disengagement becomes churn.
Loyalty — reward value and encourage advocacy.
