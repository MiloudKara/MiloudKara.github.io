02 / MATCHES

MATCH DISCOVERY

Everything happening on the pitch. In one view.

The match area is built around quick scanning: fixtures, competition context, current state and the information fans need before deciding where to dive deeper.

03 / LIVE MATCH

MATCH CENTRE

Follow the match as it unfolds.

A focused match view connects score, events, lineups and performance data so the experience stays useful from kickoff through the final whistle.

04 / NEWS & EDITORIAL

EDITORIAL LAYER

Stories that add meaning to the numbers.

Editorial content gives the platform its voice, combining timely reporting with deeper stories, analysis and football culture beyond the live feed.

05 / PLAYER INTELLIGENCE

PLAYER DISCOVERY

Know the player behind the performance.

Profiles combine identity, form and performance signals to make player discovery more useful for fans who want to understand how a player is shaping a season.

06 / CLUBS

CLUB DISCOVERY

Follow the teams that matter to you.

Club pages create a consistent entry point for fixtures, identity, competition context and the latest stories around each team.

07 / PERSONALIZATION

PERSONALIZED EXPERIENCE

Make the platform feel like yours.

Onboarding uses interests such as clubs, competitions and players to shape a more relevant home experience, making discovery feel personal from the first session.

08 / MOBILE ECOSYSTEM

FROM DESKTOP TO POCKET

The game stays with you wherever you are.

The product extends into mobile through faster match access, compact updates and content built for fans who move between the web, notifications and the matchday moment.

OUTCOME

A football product built around how fans actually follow the game.

Total Football connects live match coverage, editorial content, player intelligence, club discovery and personalization into one experience designed to make following football feel deeper, faster and more connected.
